tenure = 24. Every index is forbidden at once. The search is fenced out of its own neighbourhood and has to take whatever is left.

candidate list = 2. Each iteration costs a twelfth as much, so the budget buys twelve times the iterations — and on this instance the result gets worse (0.449 against 0.499). With only 24 neighbours, sampling two of them is noise. On your 80-site instance neighbours are far more alike, so sampling is expected to win instead. That contradiction is the point: the list size is an empirical question about your instance, and M1 asks you to answer it.
