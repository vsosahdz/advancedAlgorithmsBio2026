Entropy — is the colony still searching?
Entropy is of the pheromone-only selection distribution. Falling to zero means every ant now builds the same subset. Comparing final objective values cannot show this: a collapsed colony still reports a number, and often a respectable one.
Turn the bounds off and watch. Measured on the course's own reference implementation, 5 000 evaluations, same seed:

with bounds    entropy 4.382 → 3.279  ·  distinct subsets 10 → 6  ·  τmax/τmin = 49
without        entropy 4.382 → 2.996  ·  distinct subsets 10 → 1  ·  τmax/τmin = 7×10¹¹

All ten ants end up constructing the identical subset. The colony is no longer searching; it is re-deriving one answer, forever, at full budget. The bounded run scores 0.7191 against 0.7091 unbounded — significant at p = 0.0001 over 30 paired seeds.

Note what the early iterations look like: removing the bounds makes the run improve faster at first. That is the trap. Rapid intensification looks like progress right up until diversity is gone.
