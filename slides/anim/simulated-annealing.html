Curve for the current temperature. As T falls it collapses toward a step function: at T→0 the search accepts improvements only and simulated annealing degenerates into hill climbing.
On the guarantee. Simulated annealing converges in probability to the global optimum only under a logarithmic schedule, T(k) = c / log(k). Set α here as slowly as the slider allows and watch how little the temperature moves in 2000 steps — the logarithmic schedule is slower still, by an amount that exceeds exhaustive enumeration on any realistic instance. The guarantee is asymptotic and operationally vacuous at a fixed budget. The geometric schedule used here abandons it deliberately, and a submission that cites the convergence proof to justify a geometric schedule is citing a theorem whose precondition its own code violates.
