Diversity over time
Diversity collapsing to zero while the best stops improving is premature convergence. A flat convergence curve alone cannot tell you that — it looks the same whether the swarm found the optimum or merely agreed with itself.
On "PSO converges". The stability readout says whether (w, c₁, c₂) sits inside the region where particle trajectories converge rather than diverge — roughly w < 1 and 0 < c₁+c₂ < 2(1+w). That is a statement about the dynamics: it tells you the swarm will settle. It says nothing about what it settles on. Set c₂ high and c₁ to zero: every particle is pulled straight to the current best, the swarm collapses, diversity hits zero, and the run is perfectly stable and perfectly stuck. Stability and optimality are different properties, and conflating them is the most common false claim made about this algorithm.
