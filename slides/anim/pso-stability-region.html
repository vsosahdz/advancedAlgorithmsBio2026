What the region is, and what it is not. Inside it, the deterministic recurrence xk+1 = (1 + w − φ)xk − w·xk−1 has both roots inside the unit circle, so the trajectory converges to the attractor set by the personal and neighbourhood bests. That is a statement about the dynamics.

It says nothing about where the attractor is. Press “c₁=0, c₂=3”: φ is unchanged, the point does not move, the verdict still reads inside — and yet every particle is dragged straight onto the current global best, diversity collapses, and the swarm stops searching. The plane cannot see that, because φ is a sum and the split between c₁ and c₂ is invisible to it.

This is the trap. "PSO is guaranteed to converge" is true of the trajectory and false of the optimum, and a submission that cites the convergence result to explain a good solution has cited a theorem about something else.
